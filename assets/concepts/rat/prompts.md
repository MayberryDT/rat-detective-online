# Rat design concepts

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. These are concept images, not completed 3D models.

Shared prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Create one standalone square concept image of an improved 3D rat detective character for a low-poly browser game. The current model is a simple brown cylinder trenchcoat, sphere head, tiny round pink snout, black dot eyes, small ears, brown fedora and thin pink tail, with no visible limbs. Develop this into a much stronger readable editable Blender character design.

Each direction below was appended after "Direction:". Each prompt ended with: Composition: ONE full-body character, front three-quarter view, entire hat ears feet and tail visible, centered large with generous margins on a neutral warm-gray seamless studio background and soft ground shadow. Polished 3D concept render with deliberately simple faceted geometry, clear material separation, suitable to actually model for a small game. Soft studio key light. No scene, no text, no labels, no watermark, no weapons, no second character, no collage. This is a design proposal, not a screenshot of the existing model.

## 01-classic-detective

Classic detective refinement: keep the compact brown trenchcoat silhouette, but add a clearly elongated rat muzzle, large pink ears protruding from the fedora, fine simple whiskers, expressive skeptical brows, coat lapels, belt and two tiny visible feet. Small paws emerge from short sleeves. Warm brown coat, taupe fur, muted pink skin. Approachable, clever, subtly funny.

## 02-scrappy-street-sleuth

Scrappy street sleuth: lean lanky anthropomorphic rat with a long angular snout, oversized ears, crooked narrow-brim trilby, hunched shoulders, rolled coat sleeves, skinny little paws, scuffed shoes, patched olive-brown trenchcoat and long expressive curling pink tail. Mischievous lopsided grin. Clearly angular low-poly geometry and asymmetrical characterful silhouette.

## 03-pocket-detective

Pocket detective: very short chunky rat with a large expressive head, rounded huge ears, small dark fedora tilted back to expose the face, pronounced rat muzzle, curious shiny dark eyes, tiny hands and splayed pink rat feet. Oversized camel trenchcoat with chunky lapels and large buttons. Long thin tail in an elegant curve. Endearing but sly adult detective personality, toy-like clean sculpted low-poly volumes.

## 04-noir-veteran

Noir veteran: tall wiry seasoned rat detective, strong angular wedge muzzle, narrow thoughtful eyes, visible large ears, charcoal broad-brim fedora, long slate trenchcoat with dramatic folded lapels and belt, one paw in coat pocket and another relaxed at side, long bare pink-gray rat tail. Restrained gray-blue and warm cream palette. Graphic angular planes, sophisticated silhouette, moody but face fully legible.

## 05-neon-city-detective

Neon city detective: athletic compact rat, confident sharp rat muzzle, prominent rounded ears with pink interiors, short plum-purple trenchcoat, mustard-gold hat band on classic fedora, cream shirt, expressive dark eyebrows, visible rat paws and little boots, elegantly curved tail. Contemporary colorful low-poly game character with bright color blocking that reads in a dark purple city. Subtle cyan rim light, no futuristic armor, retain classic detective clothing.
