## 01-subtle

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a restrained 3D character upgrade mockup for Rat Detective browser game.
Reference image 1 is the CURRENT GAME screenshot and is the PRIMARY constraint on shape, proportions, simplicity and colorful materials. Reference image 2 is the LOGO and is a SECONDARY reference only for subtle muzzle, fedora, eye and collar shape cues. This is an evolutionary update of screenshot rat, not a new character.
Direction: The most conservative upgrade: retain the small round head and black dot eyes, extend the muzzle only slightly into the logo's gently angular profile, make ears a little more circular and visible, give the fedora a shallow pinched crown and add just two small angular collar planes. Nearly identical original silhouette.

Keep long thin bare pink curved tail, simple flat color materials and low polygon browser-game geometry. Do not turn into realistic or elaborately sculpted anthropomorphic detective. Do not copy logo's black palette.
