# Paperwork race, city lighting and combat follow-up

September 9, 2026, late Pacific evening. Local changes on `codex/dispatch-assignments`; no Git commit or production deployment. This follows the human review of the earlier ricochet/lighting preview and supersedes its case speed, Chain win condition and several UI details.

## Implemented

- **Chain of Custody:** each rat earns one personal delivery point for entering the active whole landmark while carrying the case. First to three wins. Carrier keeps the case; the next random landmark activates immediately. A six-landmark shuffle bag includes Icebox, Sewer Maintenance, Pump Station, Needleworks, West Sluice and Records Bureau, then reshuffles if necessary without repeating the last building immediately. The match does not require six deliveries. Personal points survive death, disarm, theft and Tampering. Existing disconnect/new-identity behavior is retained. The HUD shows the top five delivery scores and your own progress; stamp boxes and shared-stamp wording are removed.
- **Bots:** assignment travel now runs at 12 on flat routes, slowing to 6.5 near goals or on stairs. Closing carriers choose routes away from visible threats and return fire. Chain carriers route through existing entrances; some pursuers intercept at the publicly advertised next landmark. Excessive Force carriers fight and strafe with supported local steps. Bots stop wasting speculative fire on a nearby case they want to collect, and avoid triggering Dispatch while carrying or actively fighting. Shared flow fields, bounded search, movement during replanning and progress-based recovery remain.
- **Combat tuning:** combat shot intervals and burst pauses divided by 1.2; shot interval 200–316.7 ms. Speculative shot/group pauses also divided by 1.2; active/quiet windows retained. Held angular aim error reduced from 4–8° to 2.8–5.6°. Reaction delay, observed-position lag, tracking lag and short last-seen follow-through remain. This is roughly 20% faster cadence and 30% less aim error, not a claim about exact live hit rate.
- **Tampering cases:** launch 145, shot redirect 160, sustained lateral floor 140; ordinary cheese balls remain 175. Short floor hops and vertical cap 10 are retained. Cases ricochet continuously and no longer enter a hidden return delay during incident recovery. All eight persist through the incident; normal expiry still removes the seven extras and restores the primary objective case. Existing quiet buzz stays.
- **Streetlamps:** nine-unit poles on paired city curb rows with nominal 24-unit spacing; gaps preserve buildings, crossings, sewer approaches, Dispatch controls and launch pads. The current preview seed has 157 lamps (76 fixed, 81 supplemental). Detailed lamps and light cones are instanced; four reused downward spotlights, with no added shadow maps, light nearby rats. Accepted low ambient illumination remains. `lighting=classic` restores the older illumination settings, not the new lamp layout.
- **Rat visibility:** existing surface-normal outline thickens from .012 to .025, opacity .10 to .22, with a slightly lighter tint. Rat materials receive a 10% color lift and emissive intensity .12 to .22, including a small neutral fill for dark coats. Outline still depth-tests, follows animation and fades/restores with death/respawn; it is not a new through-wall rat marker.
- **UI:** Closing Time moves to the top left. Case broadcasts, case-kill and delivery confirmations move above the aiming area. Projected case/destination text avoids the center; compact scorecards leave the crosshair clear. Confirmation temporarily suppresses the competing pickup bulletin. Death/victory each have twelve shuffled phrases, without immediate repeat across bags. Popcorn copy is now “Your shots burst into a shower of bouncing cheese”; other incident copy describes player-visible action.
- **Dispatch:** larger alternating red/blue roof domes and glow. One nearest-machine voice gives a 1.6-second whoop every four seconds, gain .38 near the machine, fading to zero at 85 units. Busy state stops both light and sound. No added dynamic lights or unbounded audio voices.
- **Big Cheese:** confirmed mismatch between visible radius and center-ray wall collision, plus an oversized approximate rat test. Large balls now sweep their actual radius against box faces, rounded edges/corners and the rat’s actual collision spheres; the earliest hit supplies collision point/normal and head/body attribution. Ordinary balls retain existing physics and ray path.
