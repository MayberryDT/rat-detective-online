# Rooms play only with a human — 3 October 2026 (local; deployed 6 October)

Tyler: the Rat Detective room must not run at all times. Since 29 September `rat-detective-preview_GameRoom` and `rat-detective-staging_GameRoom` had each been active 3600 s an hour with 0 WebSocket messages, writing about 95 SQLite rows a second (about 8M rows a day each, some 16 USD a day for the pair). The cause was `GameRoom.keepsPersistentCity()` (`matchRoom === DEFAULT_ROOM_NAME`): with the persistent roster on, the canonical city kept its tick (`chaosTimer`), its 6–9 bots, its bot-heartbeat alarm and its companion feed alive with zero humans.

## Change (`src/worker/GameRoom.ts`)

- `keepsPersistentCity()` and `cityShouldRun()` are gone. The one gate is `humanSlots() > 0`: retained non-bot players (joined, or within the 30 s reconnect grace) plus admitted sockets still joining. It holds for every room and every deploy, `public-live-v2` included. There is no flag to restore the always-on city.
- `rebalanceBots()`: with no human the bot target is 0. The room removes its bots, calls the new `stopPlaying()` (disposes the bot controller, clears `chaosTimer`, flushes the city recorder, clears the heartbeat), checkpoints once if it was running, resets the round's bot count and retires from the matchmaker. For the canonical room `Matchmaker.retire` keeps the directory row and the companion feed is withdrawn.
- `activatePersistentBots()` returns without a human seat, so neither the constructor (hydration), the alarm, `ensurePersistentBots()` nor `enableMatchmaking()` can start bots. That covers the trusted routes: `GET /status`, the title's `prepare=1` socket, the admin API and the capacity `/status`. `finishJoin` brings the bots in non-match rooms too.
- The tick stops itself when no retained human remains (match rooms rebalance; others call `stopPlaying()` and checkpoint).
- The bot heartbeat alarm (`scheduleNextAlarm`), and the bounded future wake after a failed alarm, are set only while a human holds a seat.
- Companion publications need a retained human, so an empty city publishes nothing.

Existing production storage migrates itself: on the first wake after deploy, hydration runs `rebalanceBots()`, which removes the stored bots, checkpoints once and sets no further alarm.

Unchanged: the 30 s reconnect grace, during which the room keeps playing and the disconnected rat stays vulnerable. The private capacity fixture (`scripts/lib/capacity-fixture.mjs`) patches only its own staged copy to keep running `graybox-benchmark-ai-` full-lobby bots for observation. That is a token-gated, expiring private Worker. Its anchors were updated to the new source.
