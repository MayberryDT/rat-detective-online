Updated September 10, 2026. ## Clarity batch — protocol 29 (Tyler, 2 October)

- **One headline at a time** (`src/ui/Headlines.ts`): every big on-screen message asks the queue before it shows. Rank: your take of the case and your own filing credit, then your death (held until you are back), then an incident's roll and title, then the rest. An outranked message is told as one compact line at the top. A join no longer announces the case; YOU'RE ON THE CASE no longer doubles the ON THE CASE stamp.
- **Incidents:** a 2.2 s title (`INCIDENT_TITLE_MS`) with the incident's drawing and its one-line rule (`INCIDENTS[].description`), then the Dispatch ledger is only a small tag (drawing, name, seconds left). All Units' radio banner is gone; its squawk stays.
- **Death recap:** the death screen says who got you and with what (from `playerDied`: `weapon`, `headshot`, `cause`, the new validated `blast`, and the incident running), with a case-red arrow toward the case. A Crossfire banked kill reads BANK SHOT · N BOUNCES.
- **Words explained once** per browser (`localStorage` `rat-detective-explained`): made, the Hunch, Ironclad, Stakeout, HELD, each Code Violation dud and the carrier's double damage.

## Quick patch after the four-human playtest — protocol 28 (Tyler, 1 October evening)

- **PAPER CHASE** is back to five deliveries (`ASSIGNMENT_TUNING.deliveryTarget`; ten ran 52 minutes). Rule: "Deliver the paperwork. First to five wins."
- **The carrier in every assignment:** whoever carries the case deals double damage (`CHAOS_TUNING.carrierDamage`, `carrierPower`), and any kill it makes heals it to full (heal cause `case-kill`, the Quick Fix flash), in PAPER CHASE and Jurisdiction as well as Excessive Force. Its balls look heavier (1.35×) in every assignment. The rule cards and the bots' rules say so.
- **Planted Evidence and Bobbleheads are removed.** Stored rooms that name them run Improper Disposal and Crossfire (`LEGACY_INCIDENTS`). The counterfeits, their trap and burst, the occluded counterfeit outline, the bots' counterfeit avoidance and mischief, the `fake-case` ball outcome, the giant heads and their hit spheres are gone. Evidence Tampering stays behind the private classic toggle (`?incidents=classic` on a local `graybox-practice-*` room); the default mode is now called `standard`.
- **Calmer incidents:** the quiet stretch after an incident is 40 s (`CHAOS_TUNING.cooldownMs`, was 21 s); an incident still lasts 25 s.
- **Harbour Master and the precinct** count anywhere inside the whole building, like every other landmark: all of the Pier 9 warehouse (office and mezzanine included) and the whole precinct house (every floor). Guidance points at the building's middle; bots come in by Pier 9's east door and the precinct's front door. Layout 7.
- **Laser** ("a downgrade": 10 kills from 67 pickups): it fires exactly like the cheese gun, one click a shot under the shared `SHOOT_RATE` (the 1 s spacing is gone, for every rat, bots included), deals 1 damage (headshots still kill; the carrier's double applies), stays hitscan with its two ricochets, and lasts 15 s (was 10). Its look is unchanged.
- **Mousetrap holds instead of kills** (Cam's idea): a rat that steps on another rat's trap is snapped and held in place for 3 s (`trapHoldMs`, `PlayerBuffs.trappedUntil`): it can turn and shoot but not move or jump, takes no damage, and nobody is credited. The shared movement step (`legScale`, `jumpBlocked`) holds humans and bots alike; Ironclad still does not protect. The trap stays clamped shut, tugging, while it holds, then re-arms 0.9 s after letting go and leaves the rat it held alone until that rat steps clear; 8 balls or 3 laser hits still break it, and breaking it frees whoever it holds, so no doorway is sealed for good. The SNAP juice plays where it catches; the held player gets a HELD card with its clock, and everyone sees HELD on the rat's nameplate. Death cause `trap` is gone; the `trap` fact's `snap` carries `holdMs`.
- **Scattershot sound:** a new five-ball cheese blast (`public/sounds/weapons/scattershot.wav`): five cheese gun shots stacked a few milliseconds apart over a cartoon WHOOMPH, made from recordings by `scripts/generate-feedback-sounds.py`. The pitched-down pistol and synthesized BLAM are gone. Nobody has listened to it yet.
- **"You've been made"** keeps the phrase and now says what it means: the card reads YOU'VE BEEN MADE · SOMEONE CAN SEE YOU THROUGH WALLS (the Hunch, a Stakeout or a Staked Out dud alike). Since protocol 29 the meaning shows only the first time in a browser.
- **Results you leave when you choose:** the round-end timeline is unchanged (`WIN_DISPLAY_MS`, 30 s, then the reset), but each human now leaves the results board with CONTINUE (or any key). The board releases pointer lock so it takes the mouse; CONTINUE re-locks it. Server path (`GameRoom`): the humans the `gameWon` frame reaches are this round's readers (`caseFile`); a `ready` message (protocol 28, validated, rate-limited) before the reset takes a reader off the list, so they start with everyone (their board shows READY and a countdown). At the reset every other reader is held (`holdReader`): record cleared, dead (`hp` 0), gone from everyone else's city (`playerLeft`) and from welcomes, with a `continue` pending event at `READING_CAP_MS` (3 minutes). Their `ready`, a resume on a new page, or the cap spawns them (`releaseReader`: a normal spawn, `playerJoined` to the others and `playerRespawn`). Bots and observers never read; a reconnecting player's 30 s reservation is unchanged. The round never waits.
- **More stats and a download:** `gameWon.report` (`RoundReport`, counted by `RoundAwards`, bounded and validated in `messageValidation`) adds the incidents rolled by incident and, per present rat, name, kills, deaths, assists (damage within `ASSIST_WINDOW_MS` of someone else's kill), damage dealt, seconds alive (kills per minute alive), case takes and longest single carry, kills by weapon (`KILL_WEAPONS`), supplies by kind and deaths by cause (`DEATH_CAUSES`). The standings show assists, kills per minute, accuracy and damage dealt; they scroll (wheel, trackpad, swipe) down to the race and the round's tallies; rows light up on hover; hovering a name, a Case File award or a race line shows that rat's whole file, and pointing at the race reads every line there. DOWNLOAD STATS saves a self-contained page in the board's style with the raw report embedded as JSON (`#round-stats`), named for the mode and time (`src/ui/roundStats.ts`), with no server round-trip.

## Incidents changed in protocol 27 (Tyler, 1 October)

Rat Race, Delayed Reaction and Clean Bill are gone. A stored room that still names one runs All Units, Crossfire or Most Wanted instead (`LEGACY_INCIDENTS`); one that names Malpractice runs Code Violation, and one that names Act of God (renamed after Tyler's playtest) runs Cheddar Shower. Their wire fields (a shot's `delayed`/`stuckUntil`), the `unstick` sound and the retired heal causes are removed; a heal cause is now `pickup` or `case-kill`. Death cause `malfunction` is removed too: Code Violation kills nobody.

- **Most Wanted:** the police searchlight follows whoever is winning the assignment (then kills). The server looks again every second (`INCIDENT_TUNING.wantedEveryMs`); on a tie the light stays where it is, and when the wanted rat dies it moves at once to the next living leader. Whoever takes the wanted rat down gets a random supply through the ordinary reward draw (`rewardSupply(id, 'bounty')`, weapons included, Quick Fix only when hurt); a self-kill or a death to the city pays nothing. Every player sees a WANTED poster slapped on the top of the screen with the target's name (YOU in red if it is you), a red WANTED stamp on the target's nameplate, and on the takedown a BOUNTY CLAIMED stamp with who took down whom and the supply's art, with a bell strike; the next target's poster waits until the stamp is gone. Both stay above the aiming area. Bots hunt the wanted rat as before.
- **All Units:** the fallen respawn among the 12 supported spawns nearest the action (the active Jurisdiction zone, or else the real case or its carrier; `src/shared/allUnits.ts`), at least 10 units away, on the one farthest from living rats. It now opens with an ALL UNITS · ALL UNITS radio banner and a police-radio squawk; each rat that respawns as backup strobes red and blue all over for 2.2 s (emissive colour only, no lights) with a prowl-car yelp; your own respawn shows a YOU'RE BACKUP card whose arrow keeps pointing at the action.
- **Scattershot:** still a five-ball fan. Each ball that hits a rat shoves it 22 u/s along the shot and 9 u/s up (`scatterShove`, `scatterLift`); shoves on one rat in one step add up to at most 60 u/s sideways (`shoveMax`). The shooter's own kick is 3.2× harder, with its own sound (since protocol 28 a five-ball cheese blast with a cartoon whoomph; a deeper gunshot and a BLAM before). A held special weapon replaces the pattern, so it does not shove.
- **Bad Ammunition** (Tyler: "funny paths that still land where you aim"): jams, duds and crooked multi-ball volleys are gone. Every trigger fires one ball whose personality is named by its shot ID (`badRound` in `src/shared/shotPattern.ts`), so the shooter's prediction, the server and every watcher agree: a **corkscrew** (95 u/s, a 0.5 u spiral five times a second, drill whine), a **snake** (80 u/s, swaying 0.6 u, slide whistle), a **floater** (34 u/s with a bob, lives 2.6 s, kazoo), a **hiccup** (stops dead in the air for a moment, then lurches on at 230 u/s, the recorded mouth pop pitched into a HIC!) or a **superball** (an ordinary flight that keeps all its speed at every bounce, capped at 175, lives 3 s, boing on each bounce). The four path personalities fly the aim line with no drop until their first contact, then become ordinary balls; they stay within about 0.7 u of the aim line, so shots still hit (`BAD_AMMO`, `steerQuirk` in `src/shared/shotBallistics.ts`). Words float over your own ball (WHEEE!, WIGGLE!, BOING!, PFFFT., HIC!). The backfire soot, cough and womp are gone; a held special weapon fires its own plain ball.
- **Code Violation** replaces Malpractice (stored rooms that name Malpractice run it). After Tyler's playtest (1 October): "no one should die from code violation … but let's give negative effects". Every supply site of every kind hops 5 u away from a rat within 7 u (at most every 0.65 s, within 12 u of home) and walks home afterwards; the Quick Fix is harder to catch ("a little bit harder"): it scares at 10 u and hops 7 u at most every 0.48 s, within 16 u of home, and still heals fully when caught. Every other supply claimed from a site during the incident comes out as a short, harmless **dud** instead of the supply (`FAULTY_KINDS`, `FAULTY_MS` in `src/shared/pickups.ts`; `PlayerBuffs.faulty`/`faultyUntil`, one at a time, gone at death and the round's reset); supplies handed over as rewards stay good: **Cold Feet** (Hot Pursuit: legs at 0.55× for 6 s), **Rust Bucket** (Ironclad: no jumping for 6 s), **Staked Out** (Stakeout: every rat, human or bot, has you on the Hunch city-wide for 8 s, and you get YOU'VE BEEN MADE), **Backfire** (Tommy Gun: it blows up in your paws with a bang and a throw backwards, sooty card for 4 s, no gun), **Short Circuit** (Laser: the room refuses your shots for 3 s, the trigger dry-clicks) and **Snapped Paw** (Mousetrap: the trap snaps on your own paw, stuck in place and no jumping for 2 s; you can still turn and shoot). Legs and jumps follow `legScale`/`jumpBlocked` in the one `RatBody` step every rat runs, and the shot gate is the room's `admitShot`, so bots get exactly the same. The claimer sees a condemned card saying what the dud does, with a clock, and hears a zap; every player gets a CODE VIOLATION line in the feed naming the rat and the dud, and the dud's name is stamped on the rat's nameplate while it lasts; Jev reads it in words (its own and other rats'). Each launch machine fills itself to bursting every 3.5–7.5 s at random, one in three as an overpressure, and any machine firing during the incident shoves rats within 11 u of its pad. Every 1.8–4.2 s an alarm pillar near a rat clangs and shoves rats within 7 u. Every Code Violation throw (machine, pillar, Backfire) first checks the whole line out to where the rat could come down from its height, plus 4 u: if any of it is over the harbour (piers included) or outside the city it turns to the nearest safe line, and with none it does not throw. On the client, kits fidget, machines and pillars rattle, and they throw sparks with a zap when close (`violation*` in `INCIDENT_TUNING`).
- **Cheddar Shower** was removed after the clarity batch playtest (2 October; "the most laggy thing in the world"). Stored rooms that name it or `act-of-god` run Big Cheese (`LEGACY_INCIDENTS`). Its archives keep `meteor` facts and the `meteor` death `env`.
